## 4. Our Process Section

**Section Title:** How We Work
**Section Subtitle:** A structured, transparent process from first conversation to production launch.

1. **Discovery & Scoping**
   We audit your business goals, technical constraints, and target users, then define a clear scope using prioritization frameworks (e.g., MoSCoW) to eliminate wasted effort.

2. **Architecture & Design**
   Our team designs the system architecture, database schema, and UI/UX — validated through wireframes, prototypes, and stakeholder review before a single line of production code is written.

3. **Engineering & Development**
   Sprint-based development on production-grade, sovereign codebases — no fragile no-code shortcuts. Continuous integration, code review, and version control from day one.

4. **Testing & Quality Assurance**
   Automated and manual QA across performance, security, accessibility (WCAG), and cross-device compatibility, benchmarked against Core Web Vitals and industry standards.

5. **Deployment & Launch**
   CI/CD pipelines push to production environments (AWS, Vercel, or private cloud/VPC) with zero-downtime deployment strategies and rollback safety nets.

6. **Growth, Monitoring & Iteration**
   Post-launch analytics, drift monitoring (for AI systems), SEO/GEO tracking, and paid media optimization to continuously improve performance and ROI.
